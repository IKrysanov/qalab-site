QALab Publisher — Privacy Policy / Политика конфиденциальности

QALab Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the QALab channels to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

  • The tool requests only the permissions needed to publish and analyse the owner's own videos: YouTube — upload and manage own videos, post comments on own videos, read own channel analytics; TikTok — video.upload, video.publish, video.list, user.info.basic, user.info.stats of the owner's account; Instagram — content publishing, comments on own media and insights of the owner's professional account. Statistics are read only for the owner's own channels and stored locally.
  • Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared with third parties.
  • The tool does not collect, sell or transfer any personal data of other users.
  • To revoke access, remove the app in your Google, TikTok or Instagram account settings.

Инструмент используется только владельцем каналов QALab для загрузки собственных видео в собственные аккаунты. Права — только публикация, комментарии под своими роликами и статистика своих каналов. Токены доступа хранятся локально, третьим лицам не передаются, персональные данные других пользователей не собираются.
